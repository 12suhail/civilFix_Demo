ABOUT CIVILFIX

Technology for a more connected community.

CivilFix is a simple demo project designed to make civic issue reporting easy to understand and accessible.

Our idea

Cities have everyday problems that citizens notice first. CivilFix provides a simple place to learn about those problems and report them.

Our goal

Encourage awareness, participation and better communication around local civic issues.
